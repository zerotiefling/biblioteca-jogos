Descrição

WELCOME TO THE 1970s SPACE RACE

Brace yourself for a thrilling VR space adventure where every button matters and survival is a puzzle.

Take on the role of a nimble astronaut inside "Zvezda" - a highly experimental capsule. Surrounded by a fully interactive cockpit and caught in a rapidly escalating Cold War conflict, you must adapt on the fly. Execute the right procedures, manage your power and life support systems, and try to land in one piece.

Good luck, pilot. You're going to need it.

KEY FEATURES

  • Ultimate VR Interactivity: A claustrophobic, realistic space capsule where you can manipulate every single piece of equipment.

  • Narrative-Driven Comedy: A captivating story with full professional voice acting that perfectly balances tension with a heavy dose of humor.

  • High-Stakes Multi-Tasking: Keep your cool and improvise while managing complex, cascading system failures during re-entry.

  • Exceptional Replayability: Multiple endings, branching paths, and unpredictable events guarantee that no two flights are ever the same.

  • Immersive Audio & Visuals: Powered by Unreal Engine, featuring crisp retro graphics and a stellar sound design that makes you feel truly disconnected from Earth.
