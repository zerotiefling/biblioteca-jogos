Descrição

Deiland is an adventure and RPG game with some special sandbox mechanics farming, crafting, building, combat and more!
It is a calm game, made of small details, with a carefully crafted visual aspect and an interesting narrative. Deiland planet is a small living world, where everything evolves according to the actions taken by you.
